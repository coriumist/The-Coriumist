01 · The premise

Capital clusters. By season, by coordinate, by invitation.

The Coriumist reads the public record of the clustering. Flight corridors, filings, rosters, permits, notices of race. Coordinates, not people. Cities, not addresses.

Inference is marked as inference. Everything else is silence.
